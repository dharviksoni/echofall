Logbook

    How the ice works

    Echo

    When a piece moves, the square it left keeps its echo for your opponent's next move. They can't land on it or slide through it. Knights can still jump over.

    Crack

    Every departure cracks the ice. One crack, two cracks. Standing on a square never harms it. Only leaving does.

    Fall

    On the third departure the square falls into the water. Nothing can land on a hole or slide across it. Knights jump over.

    Crown

    No check and no checkmate. Capture the king to win. A player with no legal move is stranded and loses. Forty moves each without a capture is a draw.

    Ideas to try

    • The sealStep a blocker out of a line. Its echo still blocks that line for one turn, so you can unmask an attack your opponent can't answer along the same line.
    • The moatSquares around a king fall as pieces shuffle past. Wear down the ice next to the enemy king until it has nowhere to step.
    • Thin groundA piece on a twice-cracked square is safe until it moves. When it leaves, the square falls and can cut off your own retreat.

    Pieces move as in standard chess. There is no castling and no en passant. Pawns promote to queens on the last rank.
